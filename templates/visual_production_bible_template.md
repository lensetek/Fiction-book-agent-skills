# Master Visual Production Bible & Prompt Codex

> **Proyek**: [Judul Cerita / Novel]  
> **Penulis**: [Nama Penulis]  
> **Genre**: [Fantasy / Romance / Sci-Fi / Mystery / Horror / Action]

1. **Sampul Buku (Depan, Belakang, Punggung)**
2. **Lembar Konsistensi Karakter (3-Angle Turnaround & Close-Up)**

## 1. Master Book Cover Art Prompts

### A. Cover Depan (Front Cover Artwork)
- **Rasio**: `2:3` (Novel / Wattpad) atau `3:4` (Cetak UNESCO/A5)
- **Lighting & Lens**: Dramatic chiaroscuro, volumetric rim light, 85mm portrait focal length, shallow depth of field.
- **Palette**: Deep Navy (`#0F1E36`), Warm Gold (`#C5A059`), Ruby Red (`#8B0000`).

#### 📋 Prompt Siap Pakai (Midjourney / FLUX / DALL-E / Pollinations):
```text
cinematic fiction book cover art, a captivating front view of [Main Character Name, e.g. a determined female knight with braided silver hair and glowing sapphire eyes] standing in front of [Key Story Location, e.g. a majestic ruined gothic cathedral under a starlit storm sky], holding [Signature Item], dramatic rim lighting, soft glowing mist, ethereal color grading, hyper-detailed textures, elegant composition leaving negative space at the top for typography, masterpiece digital painting, trending on Artstation, 8k resolution --ar 2:3 --v 6.0
```

### B. Cover Belakang & Ilustrasi Latar (Back Cover & Moodboard)
- **Rasio**: `2:3` atau `3:4`
```text
atmospheric fantasy background landscape of [Key World Environment, e.g. misty pine forest at dusk with distant glowing lanterns], moody ambient lighting, subtle mysterious silhouettes in fog, muted color tones, clean central negative space for synopsis blurb and ISBN barcode, cinematic digital painting, 8k resolution --ar 2:3
```

## 2. Character Consistency & Turnaround Sheets (Lembar Konsistensi Tokoh)

Gunakan formula prompt ini untuk menghasilkan karakter yang **konsisten** dari berbagai sudut pandang sebelum membuat ilustrasi bab, komik, atau video micro-drama:

### A. Tokoh Utama (Protagonist Sheet)
- **Nama Tokoh**: [Nama Tokoh Utama]
- **Usia & Postur**: [Usia, tinggi badan, perawakan atletis/ramping/kekar]
- **Wajah & Rambut**: [Bentuk rahang, warna mata, gaya rambut spesifik]
- **Pakaian Ikonik**: [Pakaian khas yang selalu dipakai di setiap adegan]

#### 📋 Prompt Character Turnaround (3 Sudut Pandang):
```text
character model sheet turnaround, full body, [Male/Female protagonist, e.g. 24-year-old Indonesian young man with messy black undercut hair, sharp dark amber eyes, wearing a dark blue tactical trench coat over a high-neck shirt], showing 3 multiple angles: front view, side profile view, and three-quarter view, neutral standing pose, white solid background, studio lighting, hyper-detailed character design, clean lines, concept art, 8k resolution --ar 16:9
```

#### 📋 Prompt Potret Wajah Detail (Face Close-Up / Avatar):
```text
close-up emotional character portrait of [Protagonist Name], intense expression of [determination / sorrow / mysterious smile], sharp facial features, detailed skin texture, cinematic soft rim lighting, dark moody blurred background, photorealistic digital art, 8k resolution --ar 1:1
```

### B. Tokoh Pendukung / Lawan (Deuteragonist / Antagonist Sheet)
- **Nama Tokoh**: [Nama Lawan / Pasangan]
- **Fitur Khusus**: [Tatapan mata tajam, senyum sinis, jas gelap, aksesori khas]

#### 📋 Prompt Model Sheet:
```text
character sheet turnaround, full body, [Antagonist, e.g. tall imposing charismatic 30-year-old CEO/Sorcerer with slicked-back raven hair, piercing grey eyes, wearing a tailored charcoal suit with a golden signet ring], 3 multiple angles: front view, side view, back view, neutral pose, plain light grey background, crisp character concept art, 8k resolution --ar 16:9
```
