## Scene Architecture (Swain's Scene & Sequel Structure)

### Part A: The Scene (Action Phase)
1. **Goal**: Explicit objective entering the scene.
2. **Conflict**: Obstacle or opponent standing in the way of achieving the goal.
3. **Disaster / Cliffhanger**: Unexpected turn of events or setback at the climax of the scene.

### Part B: The Sequel (Reaction Phase)
4. **Reaction**: Emotional visceral response to the disaster.
5. **Dilemma**: Weighing options (no good choices remaining).
6. **Decision**: Choosing the next course of action (setting up the next scene's goal).
